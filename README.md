# Welcome to the Ed-Tech Projects Repository

*Welcome, students! I'm thrilled to have you here.*

This repository contains all the projects you will be working on and exploring throughout your educational journey with us.

## Getting Started

To access your specific project materials, please follow the steps outlined below:

### 1. Select Your Branch
Before proceeding, you must switch to the branch assigned to your specific batch. 
> **Note:** Because there are multiple batches, you will find the projects and assignments tailored specifically to your cohort only on your corresponding branch.

### 2. Locate Your Files
Once you have checked out your batch's branch, explore the directory to find the relevant files, instructions, and resources for your assigned project.

### 3. Start Coding
With your project files in hand, you are ready to start building, exploring the codebase, and completing your tasks!

---

*If you encounter any issues or have questions regarding your project assignments, please reach out. Good luck and have fun building!*
